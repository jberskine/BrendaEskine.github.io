ArcBest Vaux MP Coupler

The MP fits perfectly inside a trailer and can be configured in thousands of ways to meet any need. Using a standard forklift equipped with the MP Coupler, the entire MP can be moved in and out of the trailer in one swift motion. With the coupler’s tight turn radius, the MP can easily be moved around the warehouse for working freight. Outside of the trailer, freight-handling operators have access to all four sides of the MP, allowing them to pull product in seconds and entirely unload in minutes. We call this swarm processing, and it creates remarkable efficiency gains compared to traditional methods, where freight is handled piece by piece and requires multiple touches and staging ahead of time
MY ROLE - QA Engineer - Led a team of Mechanical Design Engineers and quality review and control throughout the design process.
